Delete your Crane City data

Bu sayfanın Türkçesi

You don't need an account to play Crane City, but your leaderboard entry is linked to a random player ID. You can ask us to delete it at any time, even if you have already uninstalled the game. In the game, Settings > Delete my data opens this page.

How to request deletion

  1. Find your player ID. Open Crane City and look at the bottom of Settings (shown when the game is connected to the internet): tap Player ID to copy it. If the game is no longer installed, the player name you remember is enough.
  2. Email us at retiverse@gmail.com with the subject Crane City: delete my data.
  3. Include your player ID (or your player name, team name and roughly your total population), so we can find the right leaderboard entry. Player names are not unique, so the ID is the surest way.
  4. We confirm and delete. We reply to confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.
  5. Uninstall the game or clear its data after sending the request. If you keep playing, the game creates a new leaderboard entry.

Send a deletion request

What we delete

  • Your leaderboard entry: player name, avatar, team name and total population score, together with the anonymous Unity player ID linked to it.

Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics) are stored under a separate random ID that is not linked to your player name or player ID, so they cannot be found and deleted one by one. They are deleted automatically when GameAnalytics' retention period ends. We may keep anonymous, aggregated numbers (for example, how many players finished level 10) that can no longer be linked to you.

Data on your device

Your progress, coins and settings are stored on your phone (your player name and avatar are also on the leaderboard, see above). To delete them, uninstall Crane City, or go to Android Settings > Apps > Crane City > Storage > Clear data. If Google backup is turned on, Android may also keep a copy in your Google account; you can delete it in your phone's backup settings.

This removes your progress and coins for good. We have no cloud save, so we cannot restore them.

Ads, purchases and your Google account

  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device settings (Google > Ads).
  • Ad and statistics consent (European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland): change your choice in the game under Settings > Privacy options.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Google Play and we never receive your payment details. Your order history is kept by Google in your Google account.

More details are in the Crane City privacy policy.
