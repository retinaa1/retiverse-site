Delete your Crane City data

Bu sayfanın Türkçesi

You don't need an account to play Crane City, but your leaderboard entry and gameplay statistics are linked to a random player ID. You can ask us to delete them at any time, even if you have already uninstalled the game.

How to request deletion

  1. Find your player name. Open Crane City and check your player name (and team name, if you have one) in Settings. If the game is no longer installed, the name you remember is enough.
  2. Email us at retiverse@gmail.com with the subject Crane City: delete my data.
  3. Include your in-game player name, your team name and roughly your total population, so we can find the right leaderboard entry.
  4. We confirm and delete. We reply to confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Send a deletion request

What we delete

  • Your leaderboard entry: player name, avatar, team name and total population score, together with the anonymous Unity player ID linked to it.
  • The gameplay statistics linked to your player that GameAnalytics stores for us (levels played, results, session and device details).

We may keep anonymous, aggregated numbers (for example, how many players finished level 10) that can no longer be linked to you.

Data on your device

Your progress, coins, settings, player name and avatar are stored only on your phone. To delete them, uninstall Crane City, or go to Android Settings > Apps > Crane City > Storage > Clear data.

This removes your progress and coins for good. There is no cloud save, so we cannot restore them.

Ads, purchases and your Google account

  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device settings (Google > Ads).
  • Ad and statistics consent (European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland): change your choice in the game under Settings > Privacy options.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Google Play and we never receive your payment details. Your order history is kept by Google in your Google account.

More details are in the Crane City privacy policy.
