Tap, stack, build

Your skyline is waiting!

In Crane City the crane swings and the block hangs in the air… Tap at just the right moment, land a perfect drop and raise your tower into the clouds! Every perfect drop grows your combo, and a chain of perfect drops kicks off a FEVER streak that multiplies your population!

  • 🌍

    Build the world's most famous cities

    From New York to Istanbul, from Paris to Tokyo and on to Roma… Build your own city across 250 levels in the shadow of the Statue of Liberty, Galata Tower, the Eiffel Tower, Tokyo Tower and the Colosseum. Every level you beat adds a new building to your map!

  • ⚡

    A new twist in every city

    • Iron blocks drop fast, glass blocks only survive a perfect landing
    • The rope stretches and shrinks, the turbo crane suddenly speeds up
    • Wind pushes your block, snow makes it slippery, fog hides the top of your tower

    Easy to start, built for masters!

  • 🎯

    Chain combos, collect stars

    Line up perfect drops to multiply your population and chase 3 stars on every level. Stars unlock new districts and cities.

  • 🧲

    Power-ups

    When things get tough, the slow-motion hourglass, the heavy anchor and the centering magnet come to the rescue!

  • 💎

    Daily treasure & collection

    Dig up a treasure chest every day and grab the coins and gifts inside. Collect 20 block themes, from pastel houses and neon skyscrapers to golden towers and space blocks.

  • 🏆

    Climb the leaderboard

    Compete with players around the world on total population and put your name at the top!
