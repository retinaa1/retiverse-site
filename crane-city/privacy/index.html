Crane City Privacy Policy

1. In short

  • You don't need to create an account or give us an email address or phone number to play.
  • Your progress (levels, coins, stars, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • We use the service providers named below for the leaderboard, gameplay statistics, ads and purchases.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coin balance, chosen player name, avatar and settings (sound, vibration, language, notifications) are kept in your device's local storage. They are not sent to us and are deleted when you uninstall the Game.

3. Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies)

a) Anonymous player ID. When you first open the Game, Unity creates a random player ID. It does not contain your name, email or phone number; it is used to tell players apart on the leaderboard and in statistics.

b) Leaderboard. Your chosen player name, avatar, team name and total population score are sent to the leaderboard and are visible to other players. We recommend not using your real name or other personal details as your player name. You can change your name in Settings at any time.

c) Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics). To make the Game better balanced and more fun, we collect in-game events through GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS):

  • which level you play and on which attempt, the result (won / lost / quit), duration, blocks placed, stars earned and boosters used,
  • leaving the Game in the middle of a level, running out of lives,
  • watching a rewarded ad and the type of product purchased (not payment details).

GameAnalytics also collects session start and end, device model, operating system version, language, game version and an approximate country derived from your IP address. A random ID is used to tell players apart; your advertising ID is not used for this. GameAnalytics processes this data on our behalf, and we only review it in aggregate to improve level design. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, statistics are collected only if you give consent.

  • Unity privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • GameAnalytics privacy notice: gameanalytics.com/privacy/

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The Game only shows rewarded ads: you choose to watch an ad (for example for extra hard hats or a second chance); the Game never opens ads on its own. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown when you first open the Game; personalized ads are only shown if you consent. You can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Google > Ads).

  • Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs are purchased through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see or store your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, which pack was bought at what price and Google's purchase verification token; this information is also passed to GameAnalytics for sales statistics.

Google Play privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Notifications

If you allow it, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (e.g. "Hard hats are full", "Your daily treasure is ready"). These are created on your device; no information is sent to a server for them. You can turn them off at any time in Settings or in your device's notification settings.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided data to us, contact us at retiverse@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Retention

Your leaderboard entry is kept for as long as the Game is available. Gameplay statistics are kept according to GameAnalytics' data retention rules and deleted when that period ends. Information on your device stays until you uninstall the Game.

9. Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to its processing. To have your leaderboard entry and statistics deleted, email retiverse@gmail.com with your in-game player name; we will respond within 30 days. To delete the information on your device, simply uninstall the Game.

Step-by-step instructions: Delete your Crane City data.

10. Security

All connections to our service providers are encrypted (HTTPS). No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page with its "Last updated" date.
