Pixel Port Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · Developer: Retiverse Games · retiverse@gmail.com

Bu politikanın Türkçesini oku

This policy explains what information Pixel Port (the "Game"), developed by Retiverse Games ("we"), handles and what choices you have.

1. In short

  • Pixel Port does not collect, store or share any personal data on our side.
  • You don't need an account, an email address or a phone number to play.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The Game contains no analytics, no advertising SDK and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game keeps the following in your device's local storage so you can continue where you left off: the levels you have reached, your coin balance, your powers, your lives and refill time, when you last opened the daily chest, which tutorials you have seen and your settings (sound, vibration, language). This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

3. What we don't collect

The Game does not ask for or read your name, email, contacts, photos, location, advertising ID or any other device identifier. It has no sign-in, no leaderboard and no chat, and it does not send gameplay statistics or crash reports to us.

4. Device permissions

The Game only uses the vibration permission, for short haptic feedback during play. You can turn vibration off at any time in the pause menu or in Settings. The Game does not request internet, storage, camera, microphone or location access.

5. Links that leave the Game

The "Privacy policy" button in Settings opens this page in your web browser. Once you are on a website, that site's own privacy practices apply.

6. Google Play

You download the Game from Google Play, and Google processes information about your download and device under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your device, Google may give developers anonymous, aggregated crash and performance reports (Android vitals); these do not identify you to us.

Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

7. Children

Because the Game does not collect personal data from anyone, it does not collect personal data from children either. If you have a question about your child's use of the Game, contact us at retiverse@gmail.com.

8. Your rights and data deletion

Since we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To erase everything the Game saved on your device, uninstall it or clear its data in your device settings (Settings > Apps > Pixel Port > Storage > Clear data). Laws such as the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK still give you the right to ask us questions about this policy at any time.

9. Changes

If a future version of the Game adds a service that processes data (for example rewarded ads, in-app purchases or gameplay statistics), we will update this policy before that version is released and explain what is collected and why. The current version is always published on this page with its "Last updated" date.
