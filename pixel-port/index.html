Clear, match, sail

The harbour traffic is in your hands!

First untangle the jammed junction: every vehicle can only move the way its arrow points, so find the right order and open the road. Then load hundreds of toy cars onto ferries of the same colour. Call the right ship at the right moment, keep the berths free and empty the pixel picture made of cars!

  • 🚦

    Untangle the jam

    Trucks, buses and cars are packed into the junction. Tap them in the right order and send them rolling down to the harbour.

  • 🚢

    Match colours, fill the ferries

    Every ferry only takes cars of its own colour. Pick which ship sails in next, fill the berths smartly and never let the harbour clog up.

  • 🖼️

    A pixel picture in every level

    The cars waiting on the quay form a picture: an anchor, a lighthouse, a whale, a pine tree… Load them all and the picture is complete.

  • ⚙️

    A new twist every 50 levels

    • Broken berths, twin ferries and covered ships
    • Toll gates that lift after a few cars
    • Chained cars, foggy quays and spinning turntables

    Relaxing levels with a challenging peak every few stages.

  • 🏗️

    Powers

    When things get tight, the Crane lifts any vehicle out of the way, the Extra Berth opens a spare spot for one ship and Bring Forward moves the cars your docked ships need to the front.

  • 🎁

    Daily treasure chest

    Open a chest every day for coins and powers, and spend your coins in the shop.
